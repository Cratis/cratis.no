# Cratis — brand and positioning

## Core promise

**Software that remembers.**

Cratis helps teams design, build, operate, and improve accountable, long-lived .NET business systems. The system keeps the facts behind its current state, carries contracts from C# to React, and gives people and tools a shared way to understand what happened.

## Category

> The open application and event-lifecycle stack for accountable .NET business systems.

Lead with the customer outcome, not the mechanism. Event sourcing matters because it preserves decisions and change over time. CQRS matters because it keeps intent and views explicit. Generated contracts matter because they prevent backend and frontend drift.

## The lifecycle

Use this order consistently:

> **Design → Build → Operate → Improve**

### Design

Cratis Studio is the paid collaborative design and runtime-understanding product. Teams model commands, facts, views, and flows together. Stage can perform supported parts of a model as a live sandbox. Studio is preview software: useful today, still moving, and never described as if every lifecycle connection or production concern is complete.

### Build

Cratis Build is the open runtime and application foundation:

- **Chronicle** owns the event lifecycle: contract, append, evolve, project, react, replay, inspect, and repair durable facts.
- **Arc** is the full-stack CQRS framework. C# commands and queries become HTTP APIs and generated TypeScript proxies, preserving continuity into React.
- **Components** renders the generated application boundary.
- Supporting foundations and edge capabilities remain part of Build rather than separate buying decisions.

### Operate

Workbench, CLI, and Chronicle MCP are open operating surfaces around Chronicle. Current capabilities vary by tool and version, so public copy links to the applicable documentation. Studio and Assurance are not intended to gate Build's available repair tooling. Business changes use domain commands or documented Chronicle operations so they remain visible in history.

### Improve

Runtime evidence returns to design. Durable facts, executable specifications, projections, and operating history help teams explain current state, test a change, and evolve without discarding why the system behaves as it does.

## Three customer-facing offers

### Cratis Build

**The open runtime.**

Everything required to build and operate the deployed system. Use product names when technical detail helps; present Build as one coherent offer when explaining the portfolio.

### Cratis Studio

**The paid collaborative design product.**

A shared modeling surface, executable preview, and runtime-understanding experience. Always state preview maturity and exportability. Never imply that Studio is required to run or repair Cratis Build.

### Cratis Assurance

**Expertise and responsibility.**

Support, architecture review, model sprints, first slices, production readiness, discovery, and workshops. The value is founder-led access to people building the stack and more than fifteen years of event-sourcing lineage—not permission to use open software. Capacity and commitments are set by the selected plan or engagement.

## Audience

The primary fit is an established team responsible for a .NET business system expected to live for years. The system contains decisions, handoffs, corrections, privacy obligations, or historical questions whose answers matter.

The technical champion is often a staff or principal engineer. The economic or governance buyer may be an engineering leader, platform lead, or architect. Write for both: concrete mechanisms for the engineer; continuity, accountability, and honest limits for the buyer.

## Experience and company promise

Cratis carries more than fifteen years of event-sourcing lineage across successive generations of .NET frameworks and production systems. This is evidence of learned judgment, not a shortcut around current product maturity.

The company is intentionally small, and Assurance is founder-led today. State that plainly while tying availability, response targets, and escalation to the selected plan or engagement.

## Fit and anti-fit

Cratis is a strong fit when:

- the product is expected to live for years;
- decisions and changes must be explainable later;
- .NET is the strategic backend and TypeScript/React continuity matters;
- several roles need to agree on domain behavior;
- event history, derived state, and operations need one coherent vocabulary.

A simpler approach is better when:

- the backend is not .NET and Chronicle's open boundary is not enough;
- the behavior is genuinely current-state-only, such as settings or reference data;
- the product is a short-lived prototype;
- a focused library solves the entire problem;
- the team does not have an accountability, contract-drift, or shared-design problem.

Saying no is part of the brand. Do not make Cratis sound universal.

## Voice

Cratis sounds like a senior engineer who respects the reader's time.

- Specific, plain, warm, and confident about limits.
- Pain before mechanism; mechanism before benefit.
- One idea per sentence.
- American English in public copy.
- No invented customers, testimonials, metrics, prices, dates, or capabilities.
- No maturity claim without a current, public fact behind it.
- No repository roll call where one coherent offer will do.
