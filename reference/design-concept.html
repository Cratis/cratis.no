The ecosystem

Three tools, one model of truth. Each one owns a stage between what happened and what people see.

01

Chronicle

The event store. Append-only history with SDKs for C#, TypeScript, Go, Elixir and Kotlin, plus change data capture from your existing databases.

02

Arc

A full-stack CQRS framework. Commands and queries defined once in C#, projected automatically into type-safe TypeScript for React.

03

Studio

A low-code, AI-infused surface for the whole pipeline — model domains, wire projections, and generate deployable apps without leaving the browser.

Cratis Studio

Double-click to implement. A visual editor that turns event models into running software.

Studio treats deterministic generation as a productivity accelerator, not a toy: a Monaco-based logic editor and drag-and-drop UI designer sit directly on top of Chronicle and Arc, so the same event model that captures what happened also drives what your users see.
