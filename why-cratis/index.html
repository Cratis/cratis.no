Fit

Evaluate fit from current evidence

Start with one product, one released profile, and one concrete job. Verify the current requirements and limitations before treating separate products as a combined path.

Evaluation questions

Choose the narrowest relevant path

Use the owning technical surface for each answer rather than an umbrella statement on this site.

01

What job are you evaluating?

Name the command, query, inspection task, or component area before choosing a product.

02

Which exact profile applies?

Record the product, package or image version, runtime, environment, and nearby limitations.

03

What did you verify?

A repository, package, example, or successful build does not establish maturity, compatibility, support, security, performance, or production suitability.

04

What remains independent?

Do not infer a required bundle or cross-product compatibility from names and links appearing together.
