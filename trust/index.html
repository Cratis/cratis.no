Trust

Trust and reporting routes

Use owning source, licenses, and documentation for technical review. Use the private route below for a suspected vulnerability.

License boundary

Chronicle and Workbench

Chronicle and its bundled local Workbench are available as MIT-licensed self-hosted software; authorized local use is separate from paid Cratis support, hosted coordination, or managed operational responsibility.

Review the exact Chronicle license, source repository, and technical documentation. This statement does not assign the same license or boundary to another product or repository.

Private reporting

Report a suspected vulnerability privately

Email oss@cratis.io with Security: at the start of the subject.

01

Identify the affected surface

Name the repository, package or image, and exact version or commit.

02

Keep the first message minimal

Describe the observed behavior, expected behavior, bounded reproduction conditions, and a safe contact route.

03

Do not send restricted data

Do not include credentials, customer data, personal data, production data, private topology, or sensitive logs in the first message.

04

No posture or timing claim

This reporting route does not state a product security posture, response time, service level, warranty, bounty, support plan, or disclosure deadline.
